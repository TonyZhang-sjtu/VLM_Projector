\caption{\textbf{Proposed ADirect projector with one local residual read.}
The global input adapter, global cross-attention branch, deterministic spatial
children, output order, and final shared layer normalization and MLP follow
ADirect. For each child $r$, its raw encoder mean $\mu_r$ is retained as a
residual and also supplies a content-derived query. A single eight-head
cross-attention operation reads keys and values projected from the raw
encoder patches of the same child. The update is
$u_r=\mu_r+\alpha\delta_r$, with a single shared learnable scalar $\alpha$
initialized to $0.1$ as a proposed starting value. Separate pre-normalizations
are used for the query and key/value memory; no local FFN, additional local
position encoding, sequence embedding, anatomical labels, or auxiliary loss
is included. The hard support is geometric, and the validity mask excludes
packed padding. The visual budget remains $17+52=69$ tokens at nominal
$128\times$ and $7+28=35$ tokens at nominal $256\times$ compression.
The specified reader adds 2,362,369 parameters. This is an untrained design
proposal, not a measured improvement or a registered implementation.}
\label{fig:adirect-localread-proposal}
